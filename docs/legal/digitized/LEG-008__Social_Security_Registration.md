## Legal notes

### Statutory basis

- **Social Security Act B.E. 2533 (1990)** — SSF. §33 (compulsory insured persons: employees aged
  15–60), §46 (contribution rate up to 5% each from employer and employee), §47 (employer deducts the
  employee share at the time of wage payment and remits **both** shares within **15 days** of the end
  of the month).
- **Workmen's Compensation Act B.E. 2537 (1994)** — WCF. §44 (employer registration), §45–46
  (employer-only annual contribution, rate by business risk classification).
- **Social Security Act §39/§84** and the WCA penalty provisions: late remittance attracts a
  **surcharge of 2% per month** of the unpaid contribution. Persistent non-remittance is an offence.

### The 2% per month surcharge is the number that matters

Late SSF contributions accrue **2% per month** on the outstanding amount — that is ~26.8% annualised.
It is not a fixed penalty that can be quietly absorbed; it compounds monthly until paid. Same
mechanism applies to late WCF contributions.

### This interlocks with the labour policies already in force

Registration from **1 June 2026** means every employee on payroll from that period is an insured
person under §33. Two consequences for existing policy:

1. **Deductions.** The only lawful deductions from wages are those in **LPA §76** — and the SSF
   employee share is one of them (§76(1), payment of tax / social security). This is a **lawful**
   5% deduction and is *not* in conflict with the standing rule that work-permit costs and punctuality
   fines may never be deducted (see the register: LEG-003, LEG-004).
2. **Sick leave.** SSF sickness benefit does not displace the employer's **LPA §32/§57** obligation to
   pay sick leave up to 30 days per year. The funds are additional, not a substitute.

### Employee registration is a separate step

This letter registers the **employer**. Each employee must be enrolled individually
(**form สปส.1-03**, within 30 days of hire) and departures notified (**สปส.6-09**, within 15 days of
the following month). Nothing in this document confirms that any employee has actually been enrolled.
