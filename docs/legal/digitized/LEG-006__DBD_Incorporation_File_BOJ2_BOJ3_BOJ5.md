## Legal notes

### The shareholding shown here is almost certainly stale — and that is expected

CEO confirmed (2026-07-29) the **intended** current structure: Mr. Anuphap exited, and **49% passed
to Lesia + Basel**, with the Thai side at 51%. Whether that was executed or only agreed verbally is
**unknown** — the open question this document forced into the light.

A share transfer only becomes visible at DBD after three separate steps:

| Step | Statute | Effect if missing |
|---|---|---|
| Written transfer instrument, signed by transferor + transferee + ≥1 witness | **CCC §1129 ¶2** | Transfer is **void** |
| Entry in the company's share register book (สมุดทะเบียนผู้ถือหุ้น) | **CCC §1129 ¶3, §1138** | Transfer **not valid against the company or third parties** |
| Updated บอจ.5 filed with the Registrar at the AGM (within 14 days) | **CCC §1139 ¶2** | DBD record stays stale — *this document* |

Since the AGM is overdue (see below), step 3 has almost certainly not happened even in the best case.

### FBA position

At 49% foreign / 51% Thai the company is **not** a "foreigner" under **FBA §4** (the threshold is
50% or more of the shares held by foreigners). So the target structure is FBA-clean on its face —
*provided the Thai 51% is a genuine holder*. Nominee shareholding is **FBA §36** (criminal:
imprisonment up to 3 years and/or fine THB 100,000–1,000,000, exposing both the Thai nominee and the
foreigner) — outside `/lawyer`'s remit, Mr. Ram only.

The presence of a **declared foreign 49%** is the opposite of a concealment structure, which is a
point in favour once the transfer is confirmed.

### AGM / annual filing chain — all downstream of this document

|---|---|---|---|
| First ordinary general meeting of shareholders (AGM) | **CCC §1171** — within 6 months of registration | ~2026-06-12 | **OVERDUE** — MC `5422d6cb` |

Penalty ceilings (Act on Offences Relating to Registered Partnerships, Limited Partnerships, Limited
Companies, Associations and Foundations B.E. 2499): failure to hold the AGM up to **THB 20,000**;
failure to file financial statements up to **THB 50,000**. These are statutory maxima — DBD commonly
settles lower. Actual exposure to be confirmed by Mr. Ram.
