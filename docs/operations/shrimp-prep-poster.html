🦐 Shrimp — how we prep it

Less waste, better flavor. Long life is frozen; we cook to order. · Buy fresh Size L head-on (Makro 118692, ~239฿/kg) · smell = sea, not ammonia · keep cold.

🔪 L1 — KITCHEN · make the bags
  1. Peel: head, shell, tail OFF. Devein. Rinse.
  2. Dry well with paper towel.

🔥 Marinated (raw → hot dishes)

  1. Thin coat of marinade — 8 ml per 6 shrimp. No lemon.
  2. 6 shrimp per bag → vacuum → blast-freeze −18°C.

🌯 Sous-vide (cooked → cold dishes)

  1. Salt only. 6 shrimp, single layer → vacuum.
  2. Sous-vide 60°C / 30 min.
  3. Straight into blast freezer — no ice water. Don't cut yet.
🧄 Marinade batch (~1 L): rice-bran oil 900 ml · garlic 70 g · parsley 30 g · thyme 10 g · salt 30 g. No acid.
🍳 L2 — LINE · to order

🔥 Marinated (hot)

  1. Take a bag → cook straight from frozen. Pan hot, until pink + firm + hot inside.

🌯 Sous-vide (cold / rolls)

  1. Keep 2 bags thawing in the fridge. Top up each morning. Write "Thawed:" date.
  2. No thawed bag? Cold running water 5–10 min (don't open).
  3. Open 1 bag = 6 shrimp = 1 dish. Butterfly each. 3 per roll, 2 rolls. Serve cold.
❄️ Frozen 30 d Thawed sealed 7 d Opened 48 h Never refreeze
🗑️ Old / torn / no vacuum / freezer-burn / bad smell → don't use · tell shift lead · log write-off.
