# Procurement Checklist — Universal Evaluation Criteria

> Apply this checklist to every procurement research task.
> Every comparison table must address all 9 criteria.
> Language: English (RULE-LANGUAGE-CONTRACT).

---

## The 9 Criteria

### 1. Price (including shipping to Rawai)

- Total landed cost, not just item price
- Include shipping, customs/import duties (if international), and any membership fees
- Convert to THB for comparison
- Note if price includes VAT

### 2. Delivery Time

- How long from order to arrival at Rawai, Phuket?
- Domestic (Makro, Lazada, Shopee): typically 1-7 days
- International (AliExpress): typically 2-6 weeks
- Local (Phuket markets): same-day or next-day
- Note if tracking is available

### 3. Voltage / Power Compatibility

- Must be 220V / 50Hz (Thai standard)
- Check wattage — does existing electrical capacity support it?
- Check plug type compatibility
- **Red flag:** listings without voltage specification, especially international sellers

### 4. Physical Dimensions

- Will it fit in the kitchen? Measure before ordering.
- W x D x H in centimeters
- Include clearance requirements (ventilation, access for cleaning)
- Weight — can the countertop/table support it?

### 5. Material (Tropical Durability)

- Stainless steel preferred (grade 304 or 316)
- Avoid mild steel, carbon steel, uncoated iron
- Consider humidity resistance for all components
- Food-contact surfaces must be food-grade

### 6. Warranty & Service Availability in Thailand

- Duration of warranty
- Is there a service center in Thailand? In Phuket?
- What does warranty cover (parts, labor, shipping)?
- Is extended warranty available?
- For international purchases: is warranty valid in Thailand?

### 7. Reviews / References

- Check online reviews (Lazada, Shopee, Google, YouTube)
- Professional reviews for equipment (restaurant industry sources)
- Ask in local restaurant community if possible
- Minimum 4-star rating with 50+ reviews preferred

### 8. Alternatives Considered (minimum 2)

- Always present at least 2 options
- Include a budget option and a quality option when possible
- Note why alternatives were rejected if fewer than 3 presented
- Consider used/refurbished as a valid alternative

### 9. Total Cost of Ownership (TCO)

- Purchase price + shipping
- Consumables (filters, oils, replacement parts)
- Maintenance costs (cleaning supplies, service calls)
- Energy consumption (watts x hours of daily use x electricity rate)
- Expected lifespan
- Replacement cost at end of life

## Red Flags (stop and escalate)

- No voltage specification on listing
- Seller has <4 star rating or <10 reviews
- No warranty information available
- Price seems too good to be true (>50% below market average)
- No delivery to Phuket/Rawai confirmed
- Food-contact equipment without certifications
