# Equipment Criteria — Engineering Constraints

> Reference document for all equipment procurement decisions.
> Updated by Procurement Agent when new constraints are discovered.
> Language: English (RULE-LANGUAGE-CONTRACT).

---

## Location

- **Country:** Thailand
- **Province:** Phuket
- **Area:** Rawai
- **Climate:** Tropical, high humidity (70-90% RH), ambient temperature 30-35C year-round
- **Implications:** Corrosion risk for non-stainless materials. Ensure equipment rated for tropical conditions.

---

## Electrical

- **Voltage:** 220V
- **Frequency:** 50Hz
- **Plug types:** Thai standard — Type A (2-prong flat), Type B (3-prong flat), Type C (2-prong round)
- **CRITICAL:** All equipment must be 220V/50Hz compatible. Equipment rated for 110V/60Hz (US/Japan standard) will NOT work without a transformer. Always verify before purchasing, especially from AliExpress or international sellers.

---

## Kitchen Space

- **Size:** Compact kitchen
- **Preference:** Countertop / table-top equipment preferred over floor-standing
- **Constraint:** Measure available space before purchasing any equipment. Request exact dimensions (W x D x H) from seller.

---

## Material Preferences

- **Preferred:** Stainless steel (304 or 316 grade) — corrosion-resistant in tropical humidity
- **Acceptable:** Food-grade plastic, tempered glass, anodized aluminum
- **Avoid:** Mild steel, carbon steel, uncoated iron — will rust rapidly in Phuket humidity
- **Avoid:** Chrome-plated surfaces — plating chips in humid environments

## Certifications

- **Required:** CE marking or equivalent international safety certification
- **Preferred:** Thai FDA (อย.) certification for food-contact equipment
- **Check:** UL/ETL certification acceptable as equivalent to CE for safety
- **Note:** Some Thai suppliers may have TIS (Thai Industrial Standards) marking — acceptable

---

## Procurement Decision Checklist for Equipment

Before recommending any equipment purchase, verify:

1. [ ] Voltage: 220V / 50Hz confirmed
2. [ ] Plug type: Compatible with Thai outlets (or adapter available)
3. [ ] Dimensions: Fits available kitchen space
4. [ ] Material: Stainless steel or equivalent corrosion-resistant
5. [ ] Certification: CE / อย. / equivalent
6. [ ] Warranty: Valid in Thailand, service center accessible
7. [ ] Delivery: Ships to Rawai, Phuket
8. [ ] Compatibility: Works with existing equipment (Merrychef e1s workflow)
