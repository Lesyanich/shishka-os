# Brand Identity

> Source: [`docs/bible/identity.md`](../../docs/bible/identity.md). The bible is the ratified version; vault summarizes for navigation.

## Project Name

**Shishka Eat Healthy**

## Slogan

> "Fresh as creation, alive like the soul"

## Location

Rawai, Phuket, Thailand — two sites ~1 km apart (see [[Operations/Locations]]).

## Target Opening

**Phase 1: March 2026** — L2 as store + L1 as kitchen.

## Vision

Become a global leader in healthy fast-food retail and restaurants, with franchise potential.

## Mission

Deliver healthy, authentic Middle Eastern, Russian, International, and Thai cuisine with a modern twist. **20% healthier calories** than competitors — low processed sugars, high fiber, healthy fats.

## Unique Selling Proposition

Customizable salad bars with nutrient calculations, superfoods emphasis (sauerkraut, buckwheat, fresh comb honey fungus), and AI recommendations for balanced meals — protein focus with iron boost, gluten-free options, etc.

## Brand Philosophy

**Shishka** (pine cone) symbolizes creation's spark — unprocessed, fresh food inspired by Flower of Life sacred geometry. Nourish body and soul with pure essence. The "Eat Healthy" mantra channels cosmic artistry.

## Core Concept: Functional Gastronomy

From "Healthy Fast Food" to **"Bio-Hacking & Comfort"** — also positioned as **"Green Insurance"** (eating healthy as a daily investment in long-term health).

The concept lives at the intersection of:

- **Bio-Hacking** — nutrient-dense meals, superfoods, functional ingredients (Lion's Mane, turmeric, fermented foods)
- **Comfort** — food that tastes great, not clinical or ascetic
- **Green Insurance** — healthy eating as long-term capital allocation

## Six Differentiators

1. **Cook-Chill Technology** — industrial food safety (L1 factory → L2 assembly), see [[Menu/Concept]]
2. **Culinary Booster System (CBS)** — pre-made flavor concentrates that ensure consistency
3. **Build-Your-Own Salad Bar** — 56 gastro-containers, "Algorithm Bowl" approach, see [[Menu/Categories]]
4. **AI-Driven Nutrition** — personalized recommendations based on dietary goals
5. **Zero-Waste Production** — salmon trimmings → tartare; bones → broth; vegetable scraps → boosters
6. **Arab-Russian Fusion** — unique positioning in the SE Asia market
