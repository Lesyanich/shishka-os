# Menu Concept

The Shishka menu is built on a **two-stage production model** (L1 factory → L2 assembly) and a **Culinary Booster System** that pre-locks flavor at L1, leaving L2 to do nothing but assemble. This is what lets one menu feed multiple sales points without sacrificing chef-level quality.

> Source: [`docs/bible/menu-concept.md`](../../docs/bible/menu-concept.md), [`docs/bible/kitchen-philosophy.md`](../../docs/bible/kitchen-philosophy.md). Vault summarizes — full chef-grade detail stays in `docs/bible/`.

## Production Model: L1 (factory) → L2 (assembly)

### L1 — Production
Motto: **"Precision & Preservation"**

- Sear proteins for Maillard reaction, stop at the **"90% Cooked"** rule (core not fully set)
- **Blast Chiller** (`L1-BL-FRZ-790-66`): +85°C → +3°C in <90 min. Locks moisture, kills bacterial window.
- **Chamber Vacuum Sealer** (`L1-VAC-500-67`): removes 99.9% of oxygen, extends shelf life to 7–10 days.
- Output: standardized, safe, nutrient-dense "bricks" — labeled, vacuum-sealed, ready for transport.

See [[Equipment/]] and [[Recipes/Production Routing]] for unit detail.

### Cold-chain transport
Working stock (3–5 day shelf life) + emergency stock (30 days for high-risk items). Strict +2…+4°C in isothermal boxes. Shift Leader at L2 rejects any batch arriving above +8°C.

### L2 — Service
Motto: **"Speed & Crunch"**

- Handles only ready-to-eat or ready-to-heat ingredients. Zero cross-contamination risk (no raw zone).
- High-Speed Regeneration with Merrychef/TurboChef ovens (NOT standard microwave).
- "90% Cooked" protein from vacuum bag → oven → plate in ~60 seconds. The oven evaporates surface moisture, restoring grilled texture while keeping inside juicy.

## Culinary Booster System (CBS)

The CBS is a **"Flavor LEGO"** framework. Instead of complex sauces made on-site, L2 receives concentrated **boosters** produced at L1, engineered to hit specific physiological triggers (umami, acid, crunch).

### Three axes

| Axis | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Foundation** (acid / fat / salt) | Balance the palate, carry flavors | Herb-infused cold-pressed oils, fermented citrus vinegars, mineral sea-salt blends |
| **B. Accents** (aroma / umami) | Depth and "craveability" without MSG or sugar | Miso-tomato umami bombs, dehydrated mushroom dust, charred onion oils |
| **C. Finish** (texture / heat / sweet) | Emotional response — crunch and kick | Superfood crunch-mix (seeds/nuts/spices), house chili-crunch, citrus-zest dust |

### Phase 1 booster menu

- **"Green Gold" Oil** — cold-pressed oil emulsified with cilantro/parsley stems and lime zest *(Foundation/Fat)*
- **"Shishka Dust"** — dehydrated fermented vegetables (kimchi/sauerkraut scraps) ground into fine powder *(Accent/Umami/Acid)*
- **"Ancient Crunch"** — toasted pumpkin seeds, black sesame, sumac (Arab-Russian fusion) *(Finish/Texture)*

### Strategic advantages

- **Consistency** — 90% of flavor is "pre-locked" in the booster. L2 staff assemble, don't cook.
- **Zero-waste** — uses high-flavor scraps (lemon rinds, herb stems, vegetable peels) for premium powders and oils.
- **Speed (TTR)** — reduces "Time to Receipt" by 30–45 seconds per bowl.
- **Food cost** — converts low-cost raw materials into high-perceived-value additives.

## Food cost target

**FC ≤ 30%** through:
- Zero-waste production
- Standardized "bricks" (no human factor at L2)
- CBS converting waste into perceived value
- Cross-utilization (one ingredient feeds 5 dishes)

See [[Menu/Pricing & Margins]] for the per-dish breakdown.

## Design constraints (every dish must satisfy)

1. **Flavor balance** — all 5 tastes + umami
2. **Nutrient density** — high micronutrient-to-calorie ratio
3. **Plating aesthetic** — visual freshness ("Instagrammability 2026")
4. **Reproducibility** — line cook can assemble fast, no quality loss

If any criterion fails, the dish is not menu-ready.
