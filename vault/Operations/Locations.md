# Locations

Source: [`docs/bible/locations.md`](../../docs/bible/locations.md). Two physical sites in **Rawai, Phuket, Thailand**, ~1 km apart.

## L1 — Kitchen + Mini-Store

- **Size**: 25–30 m², 2 floors (balcony with mountain view)
- **Rent**: 18,000 THB/month (paid for 1 year)
- **Address**: Rawai, Phuket
- **Role**: central kitchen — produces all batches, ships to L2

### Phased development

| Phase | What L1 becomes |
|---|---|
| **Phase 1** (now, March 2026) | Prep kitchen only — cutting, cooking, bottled sauces, sourdough bread |
| **Phase 2** | Open mini-store with outdoor seating (roadside) and 2nd-floor seating (mountain view) |
| **Phase 3** | Add 3rd-floor seating + morning yoga classes (general manager leads). Work permits in process |

### Layout (Phase 1)

L1 is organized into 4 zones — full equipment list in [[Equipment/Inventory]]:

- **Zone 1 — Logistics & Storage** — pantry, fridges, vacuum-bag stock
- **Zone 2 — Cold Prep** — UV-filtered sink, slicer, bowl cutter, dough mixer
- **Zone 3 — Hot Production** — gas range, lava grill, convection oven (under extraction hood)
- **Zone 4 — Cook-Chill Hub** — Blast Chiller (the bottleneck), Vacuum Sealer, Assembly Pass

## L2 — Sales Point at Tops Daily

- **Size**: 32 m²
- **Rent**: 20,000 THB/month (paid for 6 months)
- **Address**: high-traffic location, ~1 km from L1
- **Role**: customer-facing — assembly, regeneration, service

### Layout

- **Two 1.5 m salad bars** (56 gastro-containers of varying sizes) with LED lighting for the vibrant "ingredient path" — see [[Menu/Categories]]
- **Assembly on-site** in <2 minutes (TTR target — see [[Finance/Targets & KPIs]])
- **Reheat station** — Merrychef / TurboChef oven for wraps, sandwiches, mains
- **2–4 tables** for eat-in
- **Open fridge** (1.2 m × 2 m) at hall end for take-away (soups, coconuts, mashed potatoes with cutlet)

## Logistics — L1 ↔ L2

- Distance: ~1 km
- Mode: motorbike, ~5 min one way
- Frequency: **3× per day** (07:30 / 11:30 / 15:30 — TBD finalized)
- Carrier: isothermal box, +2…+4 °C
- Reject criteria at L2: any batch arriving above +8 °C → batch rejected, logged in HACCP sheet, owner notified

## Phases of development (global)

```
Phase 1  (March 2026)  L2 = main store, L1 = kitchen only
Phase 2                L1 mini-store opens — roadside + 2nd-floor seating
```
