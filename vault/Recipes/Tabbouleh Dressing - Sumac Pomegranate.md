# Tabbouleh Dressing — Sumac & Pomegranate

A bright, tangy dressing built on lemon and pomegranate molasses, with sumac for that signature tart-citrusy edge. Makes enough for one large bowl of tabbouleh (4–6 servings).

## Ingredients

Total yield: ~162 g

| Ingredient | Grams |
|---|---|
| Extra-virgin olive oil | 80 g |
| Fresh lemon juice | 45 g |
| Pomegranate molasses | 20 g |
| White wine vinegar | 10 g |
| Ground sumac | 4 g |
| Fine sea salt | 3 g |

## Method

1. In a small bowl or jar, whisk together the **lemon juice, vinegar, pomegranate molasses, sumac, and salt**. Let it sit 2–3 minutes so the sumac blooms.
2. Slowly **drizzle in the olive oil** while whisking (or seal the jar and shake) until it emulsifies into a slightly thick, glossy dressing.
3. **Taste and balance:** adjust lemon for sharpness, salt if flat.
4. Toss with the tabbouleh **just before serving** so the parsley stays vibrant.

## Notes

- **Weight ratio:** oil : total acid (lemon + vinegar) ≈ 1.45 : 1, molasses ≈ 36% of lemon weight, salt ≈ 1.8% of total. Multiply every gram figure to scale.
- Keeps in the fridge **up to 5 days**; whisk again before use as it separates.
