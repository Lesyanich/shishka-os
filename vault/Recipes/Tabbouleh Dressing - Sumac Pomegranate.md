# Tabbouleh Dressing — Sumac & Pomegranate

A bright, tangy dressing built on lemon and pomegranate molasses, with sumac for that signature tart-citrusy edge. Makes enough for one large bowl of tabbouleh (4–6 servings).

## Ingredients

- **6 tbsp** extra-virgin olive oil
- **3 tbsp** fresh lemon juice (about 1½ lemons)
- **1 tbsp** pomegranate molasses
- **1½ tsp** ground sumac
- **½ tsp** fine sea salt (adjust to taste)
- *Optional:* ¼ tsp ground cumin, pinch of Aleppo/chili flakes, ½ tsp honey, pomegranate seeds for garnish

## Method

1. In a small bowl or jar, whisk together the **lemon juice, pomegranate molasses, sumac, and salt**. Let it sit 2–3 minutes so the sumac blooms.
2. Slowly **drizzle in the olive oil** while whisking (or seal the jar and shake) until it emulsifies into a slightly thick, glossy dressing.
3. **Taste and balance:** more lemon for sharpness, a touch of honey to round it out, more salt if flat.
4. Toss with the tabbouleh **just before serving** so the parsley stays vibrant.

## Notes

- **Ratio to remember:** 2 parts oil : 1 part acid (lemon), plus the molasses for depth. Scale freely.
- The **pomegranate molasses** is what makes it sing — sweet-sour and dark. If unavailable, reduce 4 tbsp pomegranate juice with 1 tsp sugar until syrupy.
- Fresh **sumac** matters — if yours is old and dull, bump to 2 tsp.
- Keeps in the fridge **up to 5 days**; whisk again before use as it separates.
